\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item A calibrated decision layer converts any fixed risk score into an auditable patient-blocked alarm policy.
\item Finite-grid empirical-Bernstein and Learn-then-Test selectors control mean alarms per patient-day.
\item Ten repeated splits per site show no mean-budget violation, and a validation-ranked selector avoids over-alarming at loose budgets, raising utility from 0.56 to 0.93.
\item Source thresholds lose sensitivity across hospitals; label-free rate matching recovers the target operating point.
\item A ward simulation turns 1 versus 2 alarms per patient-day into 3.8\% versus 12.7\% hours over capacity.
\end{itemize}
