\section*{Data and code availability}
The raw PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2019 records are publicly available from PhysioNet under its data use agreement and are not redistributed with this manuscript. The analysis code, subject-split audit, score-cache builders and experiment drivers are included in the accompanying repository. Derived score caches and the bootstrap outputs are kept outside the manuscript repository because they can be regenerated from the public records and the documented commands.

\section*{Ethics statement}
This study used only the public, de-identified PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2019 cohort. No new patient data were collected, and no intervention or prospective clinical evaluation was performed. The analysis is a retrospective methodological study and does not constitute a clinical validation or a deployment recommendation.
