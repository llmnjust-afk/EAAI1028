\section*{Data and code availability}
The raw PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2019 records are publicly available from PhysioNet under its data use agreement and are not redistributed with this manuscript. The analysis code, subject-split audit, score-cache builders and experiment drivers are included in the accompanying repository. Aggregated result files, including the seed-level bootstrap outputs and the revision analyses, are archived under \texttt{code/experiment\_results/} and \texttt{results/}; raw records and score caches are not redistributed and can be regenerated from the public records with the documented commands.

\section*{Ethics statement}
This study used only the public, de-identified PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2019 cohort. No new patient data were collected, and no intervention or prospective clinical evaluation was performed. The analysis is a retrospective methodological study and does not constitute a clinical validation or a deployment recommendation.
